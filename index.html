Photographing your sample

  • ☀
    Bright, even light. Natural daylight, no shadows or flash glare.
  • 🎥
    Sharp focus. Blurry is the #1 reason a sample fails to process.
  • 📏
    Camera flat, straight above. An angled shot stretches the letters.
  • ✍️
    Space letters out. Keep strokes from touching -- avoid cursive.
  • 📄
    Normal size, flat page, solid pressure. Faint strokes can get lost.
  • 🖊️
    A fine pen. Fineliner or fine ballpoint traces most cleanly.

See it for yourself

Your custom font is a careful re-creation of your handwriting, not a perfect trace -- small natural variation is added on purpose so pages don't look robotically repeated. Expect it to be close to your hand, not a 100% match.
